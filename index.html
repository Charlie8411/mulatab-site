Product

Everything the money person carries — in one place.

No more screenshots of receipts, mental math at the table, or chasing people over text. Mulatab handles the whole loop, from scan to settled, in dollars.

Receipt scan

Point your camera at any receipt. Mulatab pulls out line items so you split by what each person actually ordered — not a flat average.

Smart Split

Even, by item, by share, or custom. Smart Split collapses the math, assigns tax and tip fairly, and lets everyone see exactly what they owe in dollars.

Recurring tabs

Rent, utilities, the streaming bundle — set a tab once and it repeats on schedule. The roommate ledger keeps itself current so nobody has to remember.

Tabs for everything

One tab per trip, dinner, or roommate household. Group balances stay separate and clear, so nobody loses track of who's in on what.

Points & rewards

Settle on time and you climb tiers. MulaPoints reward the people who square up fast — a gentle nudge that keeps tabs from going stale.

Recap cards

Shareable summaries of a trip or month — what was spent, who covered what, and how it all evened out. The receipt for the whole story.

FAQ

The quick questions.

Is the demo using real money?
No. The Mulatab prototype runs in safe demo mode — every balance and transfer is simulated, and no real money moves. It's built so you can experience the full flow before launch.
What can I do in the live demo?
Walk the full loop: scan a receipt, split it with Smart Split, send and request money between friends, settle a tab, and earn rewards points. Open it on a phone and add it to your home screen for the full experience.
How are amounts displayed?
Everything is dollar-first. Balances, splits, and settlements are all shown in USD so the whole crew is always reading the same numbers.
When is the app coming?
An iOS beta is targeted for June 2026. Join the waitlist above and pilot-ready groups go first — plus you'll get the investor brief.
